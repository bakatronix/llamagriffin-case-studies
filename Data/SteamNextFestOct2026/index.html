Steam Next Fest October 2026The Largest — and the Most Split — Edition Yet.

The October 2026 edition ran 19–26 October with 7,240 games taking part — the largest Next Fest in the program's history, up 65% on June's 4,382 and roughly eight times the 887 of June 2023.

That scale crossed a threshold: for the first time, a festival more than half of the year's Steam releases flowed through. And it changed what the event does — it stopped creating breakouts and started amplifying the audience a game already had, measured the day before it even began.

This case study tells that story in three parts — the record scale, who actually got seen, and what predicted early success — and closes with why this edition was a genuinely different animal.

Why this edition was different
I
A different species of scale
Not just a record — a threshold. Entries hit ~51.7% of all projected 2026 Steam releases, up from 38.2% in 2025 and 33.6% in 2024. Next Fest went from something a third of releases used to something most of them use.
II
The two-tier field went from trend to headline
Past editions looked for a single breakout. This one made the split unavoidable: ~100 games held real audiences, and ~6,900 arrived with a median of 15 followers — the gap decided before the first slot was served.
III
AI disclosure became a structural layer
35% of games disclosed generative AI, concentrated at the bottom (8% of the top 25 vs 35% of everyone else). For the first time, "slop" could be charted as a measurable feature of the field, not hand-waved away.
IV
An idle/desktop vein cracked the top
Character House, Find The Needle and Melvor Idle 2 reached the leaderboard on long-session retention — holding numbers while the early-fest press demos decayed to a tenth of their peaks. A new leaderboard shape, not the old spike-and-fade.
