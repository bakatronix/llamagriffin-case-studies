Steam Next Fest October 2026The Largest Edition On Record.

The October 2026 edition ran 19–26 October with 7,240 games taking part — the largest Steam Next Fest in the program's history, up 65% on June's 4,382 and roughly eight times the 887 of June 2023.

The real story is what scale did to the field: the festival stopped creating breakouts and started amplifying the ones that arrived with an audience already built. Early-festival data shows pre-fest follower base and pre-fest momentum predict early peak players about equally — while last-minute percentage growth from a tiny base does not.

This case study tells that story in three parts: the record-breaking scale, who actually got seen, and what predicted early success.
