So What?

What This Means for Players

A clean test of storefront discipline.

Riot publishes its regional VP prices and adjusts them openly — it is the most transparent repricing behaviour on this desk. When Turkey's lira weakens, Riot eventually updates the TRY price. The window between currency movement and repricing is the period where the implied discount is largest, and this page tracks it daily.

Valorant Points cannot be transferred or refunded. The skin you buy stays on the account it was purchased on. The price gap between Philippines and UK for the same cosmetics budget is the full spread of the regional pricing decision Riot makes, recalculated every morning.

Section 05

Methodology

How this index is built.
  • US price: Roblox web store. Confirmed current. Tagged Official.
  • Regional prices: FoxReload distributor data, May 2026. Tagged Reseller-reported or Contested until direct storefront verification. India prices conflict across sources and are flagged.
  • Exchange rates: Frankfurter. Daily mid-market rates from 84 central banks, fetched on every page load. Falls back to a stamped snapshot if the fetch fails.
  • Storefront: Web store prices only. App-store purchases carry a platform commission and are excluded.
  • It does not claim Roblox is mispricing anything. Regional pricing is deliberate purchasing power adjustment.
  • Part of any gap reflects that policy working as intended, alongside app-store matrices and local tax treatment.
  • Robux cannot be transferred between regional accounts. These gaps are not a practical arbitrage route.

Since November 2024, $9.99 on the Roblox web store yields 1,000 Robux due to a 25% bonus. The 800-Robux SKU remains a distinct purchasable tier and is still the labelled bundle in most regional storefronts.

This index benchmarks the 800-Robux bundle. The distinction is intentional: the index measures the bundle price, not the effective yield after bonus.

  • Roblox-set local prices: Roblox denominates the purchase in local currency for certain markets. These appear in the main table.
  • USD-billed with local tax: Markets like Argentina are billed in USD. Local banks apply import taxes and withholding at checkout. This is tracked separately in the Argentina note, not the main table.
Benchmark bundle: 950 VP base (950 + 50 standing bonus = 1,000 total) via Riot web store. Baseline: United States at $9.99. Brazil benchmark uses the 1,120 VP base tier (closest available).. Section 06

The Reframe

The currency that buys every skin.

Riot deliberately publishes its regional VP prices and adjusts them as an explicit pricing tool. When the lira falls, the TRY price holds until Riot updates it. When it updates, it moves in steps. Every step is a decision: who pays more, who pays less, for the same cosmetic. The Big Mac Index endured for forty years because it turned purchasing power into a sandwich. This page does the same with the currency that buys Valorant skins. Not what 950 VP costs. Who is asked to pay the most, and how deliberately that gap was set.
